# Branches

A passport is split into branches, each with its own EP and its own level, and nothing adds them up.

Published at https://noventia.xyz/docs/branches. Updated 2026-11-10.

A passport has no total. It has branches. Each branch counts its own kind of fact, earns its own EP and has its own level. Nothing adds the branches up into one number: [No single score](rules-we-hold.md) is one of the rules Noventia holds.

| Branch | Facts that earn EP | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Holder | Airdrops kept for 30 and 90 days after the claim; native SOL staking and liquid staking tokens held for 30 and 90 days | Live |
| Trader | Active trading weeks: at least 3 swaps the wallet signed, on at least 2 days of one week | Live |
| Builder | Programs deployed; votes cast in DAOs | Live |
| LP | Liquidity kept in a pool | Planned |
| Early | Use of a protocol before its token | Planned |

Holder, Trader and Builder run on the passport at [app.noventia.app](https://app.noventia.app) and read Solana. LP and Early are named. Their rules are not written yet.

## EP and levels

* EP comes only from facts, each with its transaction. See [Facts and proofs](facts-and-proofs.md).
* Each branch has its own five levels, L1 to L5.
* There is no total across branches.
* The EP values of the facts are a draft.

## Looks automated

On the Trader branch, a wallet that looks automated shows the mark "Looks automated". It earns no Trader EP until an appeal is decided.

## Linked wallets

A project's list check finds groups of linked wallets: wallets of at least 3 owners that two or more independent signals link. A signal is a fact, such as the same source sending the first SOL, funds sent to the same wallet, the same programs called in the same order, first use shortly before the snapshot, or sales and moves out on the first day of past airdrops. Every signal counted for a wallet carries that wallet's own transactions. Signals that rest on the same link count once, so a common funder and a burst of fundings from that source are one signal. An exchange, a bridge, a launchpad or a program account does not count as a common funder: see [What we recognize](https://noventia.xyz/docs/what-we-recognize). The wallets linked to one passport count as one owner, so a person's own wallets do not count against each other. A shared funder alone gives no label and no freeze. Every group labeled today has a common funder and at least one more signal.

The list check labels such a wallet "Linked wallets: some evidence" when two signals agree, and "Linked wallets: strong evidence" when three or more agree. The evidence is a category, with no number. Each label shows its signals and their transactions. The thresholds of each signal are not published.

A wallet in a group of linked wallets is frozen. It earns no new points in any branch until a review is decided, and keeps the EP it earned. The freeze applies to that wallet only. To contest it, use Appeal on the passport, or write to [support@noventia.app](mailto:support@noventia.app) with the case id from the freeze.

## Appeals

Every freeze has an appeal. Press Appeal on the passport, or email [support@noventia.app](mailto:support@noventia.app) with your Noventia ID. Noventia decides each appeal. See [Cases and the council](cases-and-council.md).
