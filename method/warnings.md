# Warnings

Four warning levels, each shown with the facts behind it, and the exact rules the developer card uses.

Published at https://noventia.xyz/docs/warnings. Updated 2026-11-12.

A warning is always shown with the facts behind it and the right to appeal. There are four levels. None of them is a total score: see [Rules we hold](rules-we-hold.md).

1. **Little history.** A new address with few facts. This level says nothing bad about the wallet.
2. **Warning signs.** Set by the rules, with the facts listed.
3. **High risk.** Set by the rules only under strict open rules with clear proof on chain, such as liquidity pulled or the creator selling right after launch.
4. **Confirmed fraud.** Only after review by the PoE council. The owner is told first and can answer first. The council is planned for stage 3, Network.

A complaint does not switch on a label by itself. With the council, it opens a [case](cases-and-council.md).

## Rules on the developer card

These are the rules of card rules version 2. The terms are defined in [Card facts](card-facts.md); N and X are in [Thresholds](thresholds.md).

* **Level 3, high risk:** at least one launch with a liquidity pull by the creator within N hours of launch, or first day dumps in at least 2 launches.
* **Level 2, warning signs:** exactly one first day dump, or any launch where the creator moved out at least X% of their own allocation on day one.
* **Level 1, little history:** a wallet younger than 90 days or with fewer than 3 launches, and no level 2 or 3 facts.

One event does not make level 3, except a liquidity pull, which is the clearest proof on chain.

## On the passport

Two rules on the passport stop points, each with an appeal:

* **Looks automated.** A wallet that looks automated earns no Trader EP until an appeal is decided.
* **Linked wallets.** A wallet is frozen when a project's list check finds it among linked wallets: wallets of at least 3 owners that two or more independent signals link. It earns no new points in any branch until a review is decided. Only that wallet is frozen. See [Linked wallets](branches.md#linked-wallets).

## Appeals

Appeals are live today: email [support@noventia.app](mailto:support@noventia.app) with the case number, or use the Appeal button on the passport. A Noventia operator decides each appeal.
