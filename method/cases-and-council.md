# Cases and the council

How to appeal a freeze or a mark today, and how the council planned for stage 3 is to decide cases.

Published at https://noventia.xyz/docs/cases-and-council. Updated 2026-11-10.

Every freeze has an appeal. Appeals run today. The council that decides cases is planned for stage 3, Network.

## Appeal today

* Press Appeal on the passport, or email [support@noventia.app](mailto:support@noventia.app) with your Noventia ID.
* Noventia, as the operator, decides each appeal.
* A wallet is frozen when a project's list check finds it among linked wallets: wallets of at least 3 owners that two or more independent signals link. It earns no new points in any branch until the review is decided.
* A wallet that looks automated earns no Trader EP until the appeal is decided.

See [Branches](branches.md) for the freeze and the mark "Looks automated".

## How a case runs

Planned for stage 3, Network, with the council. These rules shape the design.

* A case starts with a complaint or with an appeal by the owner.
* The evidence is facts, each with its proofs.
* The owner is told first and answers first.
* The council decides. Only the council can set warning level 4, confirmed fraud. See [Warnings](warnings.md).
* One case does not end a passport. Patterns matter.
* A complaint alone does not switch on a label. It opens a case.

## Who the council is

The council is planned for stage 3, Network. Until it opens, Noventia decides appeals as the operator. Council nodes are planned to run the trust network on the Solana Virtual Machine: see [Attestation](https://noventia.xyz/docs/attestation). A seat on the council for [Pioneers](https://noventia.xyz/pioneers) is planned as well.

## What a case record holds

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `caseId` | The case |
| `subjects` | The passports or wallets it is about |
| `evidence` | Facts, each with its proofs |
| `status` | Where the case stands |
| `decision` | The outcome |
